These three Paris hotels are not a ranking and they are not a price list. Jeanne d’Arc is a 3-star the hotel itself calls economical, on a Marais side street. Henriette is a small Left Bank house with a courtyard. Fabric is a former workshop between République and Bastille that the hotel files as a 4-star. Open Booking.com for the live rate.
