Spain

The grid, and one house off it.

Two of these hotels sit in the Eixample, where a townhouse can look like a much bigger budget. Praktik Rambla is on Rambla de Catalunya, a few minutes from Plaça Catalunya. Casa Bonay is on Gran Via. Hotel Brummell is the turn off the grid: 20 rooms in Poble Sec, an 1870 facade, and a pool the hotel expects you to use. Rates stay on Booking.com until this desk has checked them.
