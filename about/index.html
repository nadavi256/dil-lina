The edit

A list with a budget, and a rule about numbers.

Lina looks for hotels that feel like a treat and often land around $80 to $150 a night, plus the occasional place that is honestly expensive. The audience is international. The cities on the desk right now areParis, Rome, andBarcelona.

The rule is stricter than the taste. A nightly rate is published only after it has been checked for a date, a room, and an occupancy. A Booking.com score is printed only with the review count and the day it was read. If a figure has not been checked, the record stays marked TODO and the button sends you to Booking.com. The rates on the site now were read on 23 October 2026 for the night of 10–11 November, two adults.

Hotel pages do not use another building’s photograph and call it the property. City pages use royalty-free photographs from Unsplash, with the photographer credited on the picture.
