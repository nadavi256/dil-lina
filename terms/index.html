How to read this site.

Lina is operated by Nadav Stern. The pages are general information about hotels and about how to look for one. They are not an offer of accommodation, and Lina is not the merchant. A booking is a contract between you and Booking.com or the hotel, on their terms.

Prices, availability, taxes, and cancellation rules change. When Lina prints a rate, the page says which night was checked. The figures now on the hotel pages were read on Booking.com on 23 October 2026 for 10–11 November 2026, two adults. Do not treat that figure, a neighborhood description, or an older article as the price you will pay on a different date. Read the booking page before you pay.

Facts on a hotel page are kept short on purpose, and they can still go stale. Official star categories are repeated only when the hotel states them, and they are not guest scores. Photographs of cities are stock images with credits. They are not photographs of the hotels.
