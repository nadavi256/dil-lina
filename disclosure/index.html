Plainly

Some links pay for the editing.

Lina participates in affiliate programs. Hotel buttons on this site go to that hotel’s page on Booking.com. The site also loads the Stay22 script, formerly called Let Me Allez, with affiliate id dillina. Stay22 can rewrite a Booking.com link so that a completed booking may be credited to Lina.

If you book through one of these links, Lina may earn a commission. The commission is paid by the advertiser. It is not added to the price you pay. You should see the same room rate you would see by opening Booking.com yourself.

Commission does not decide which hotels are listed, and it does not create a price or a Booking.com score. A rate is printed only after it has been checked. Hotels are chosen because they fit the edit: a striking room, a useful neighborhood, and, sometimes, a true-luxury name kept on the list for contrast.

A short version of this notice sits in the footer, beside the other links, and again next to every Check prices button.
