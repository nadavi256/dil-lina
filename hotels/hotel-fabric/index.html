Hôtel Fabric

Oberkampf, 11th arrondissement

No stand-in photograph

Fa

Hôtel Fabric occupies a former textile factory at 31 rue de la Folie Méricourt, in the 11th, between République and Bastille. The hotel says the house has 33 rooms on four floors and files itself as a 4-star, with an interior drawn from the workshop past of Oberkampf: brick, volume, and large windows. It looks like a much louder luxury hotel. Whether tonight’s rate belongs in Lina’s range is a question for Booking.com, not a number we will guess.
