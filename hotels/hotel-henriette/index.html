Hôtel Henriette

Gobelins, 13th arrondissement

No stand-in photograph

He

Hôtel Henriette stands at 9 rue des Gobelins, a short walk from the Mouffetard street and the Latin Quarter. The hotel describes a small Left Bank house: 32 bedrooms designed individually, a flowered courtyard, and a desk that stays open through the night. It is the kind of address that looks considered without pretending to be a palace. Lina has not checked a live rate, so none is printed here.

What is actually known

  • 9 rue des Gobelins, 75013 Paris
  • 32 individually designed rooms, according to the hotel
  • Interior courtyard
  • A short walk from Les Gobelins metro
