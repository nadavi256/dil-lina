G-Rough is a member of Design Hotels at Piazza di Pasquino 69, a few steps from Piazza Navona. The hotel’s own account describes a historic palazzo you enter through a wine bar rather than a formal lobby, and a Roman founder’s idea of an apartment you are handed the key to.
