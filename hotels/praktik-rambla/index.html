PR

Praktik Rambla is at Rambla de Catalunya 27, in the Eixample. The hotel places it a few minutes on foot from Plaça Catalunya and about a minute from Passeig de Gràcia. The public listing describes a townhouse interior with original columns and tiled floors. Lina is interested in that kind of room: old bones and a central pin. The rate printed here is the Double Room Booking.com showed for two adults on 10–11 November 2026.
